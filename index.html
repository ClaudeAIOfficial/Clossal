In Partnership with DTCare

America’s Favorite Pet partners with PAWS (Progressive Animal Welfare Society) to aid in its mission to help cats, dogs, and wild animals thrive in happy, healthy homes or in their natural habitats. Since 1967, PAWS has unified more than 130,000 cats and dogs with loving families, nurtured more than 140,000 sick, injured, and orphaned wildlife, and made the world a better place for countless critters.

Modern Dog & Modern Cat

Each year, America’s Favorite Pet has given one fine feline and one handsome hound the opportunity to shine on newsstands. In 2023, Hunter’s life was forever honored in print, while Oakley’s story of survival was shared with cat lovers across the nation. The top cat and dog will not only get prime real estate on the cover of Modern Cat and Modern Dog Magazines but also a two-page spread to highlight their own unique journeys.

FAQ

How does America’s Favorite Pet support PAWS?
America’s Favorite Pet is an annual competition and fundraised operated by Clossal, benefiting PAWS.
In 2024, the campaign resulted in a $8.9M grant to PAWS through DTCare.

What is America’s Favorite Pet?
America’s Favorite Pet is a nation-wide competition in search of one dog and one cat to be featured on the cover of Modern Cat / Modern Dog magazine and take home $10,000 each!

For inquiries regarding the competition rules, terms, or privacy policy, please contact support@americasfavpet.com.
